# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Startup founders and early product teams that need an end-to-end engineering partner to turn an idea or an evolving product into production software.

## Product Purpose

J Nova Systems provides product engineering services across web applications, mobile applications, SaaS platforms, UI/UX, APIs, and cloud delivery. The website must help a founder understand the offer, evaluate the company’s approach and work, and start a project conversation.

## Positioning

J Nova Systems is a focused product engineering partner that connects product thinking, interface design, software development, and cloud delivery as one continuous engagement rather than selling isolated implementation tasks.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients evaluate the company through its service capabilities, selected project work, delivery approach, engineering insights, and project inquiry form. The primary conversion is a qualified project inquiry.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Web application development
- Mobile application development
- SaaS platform development
- UI/UX design
- API design and implementation
- Cloud delivery
- The existing Projects and Blog data feeds remain in use under company-oriented sections.
- Contact submissions must be stored in Cloudflare D1 and emailed through a Cloudflare Email Worker binding to `developers@jnovasystems.com`.
- The site uses Next.js and deploys through OpenNext on Cloudflare Workers.
- The site must remain responsive, keyboard accessible, and usable with reduced motion.

## Brand Commitments

- Company name: J Nova Systems
- Company-first language replaces the current personal portfolio and freelancer framing.
- Voice: clear, capable, direct, and technically credible for startup founders.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing project records provided by the Projects data feed.
- Existing articles provided by the Blog data feed.
- No verified customer logos, testimonials, performance metrics, awards, pricing, or market claims are currently available; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make the complete product-engineering offer immediately understandable.
- Demonstrate connected delivery rather than listing disconnected skills.
- Earn trust with real work and a clear process, not unsupported claims.
- Keep project inquiry direct, reliable, and transparent about submission state.
- Preserve speed and usability while delivering a distinctive technical experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must preserve semantic structure, keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and a reduced-motion alternative for dimensional effects.
